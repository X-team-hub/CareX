Why track your cycle?

  • Keep a record of previous periods.
  • Understand your usual cycle and period duration.
  • Review symptoms over time.
  • Support estimated future period dates when enough history is available.

How cycle length works

Cycle length is counted from the first day of one period to the first day of the next period. It is different from the number of days bleeding lasts.

For example, a 28-day cycle does not mean a period lasts 28 days.
